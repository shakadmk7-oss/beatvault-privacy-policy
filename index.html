Beat Vault Music Player — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Everything stays on your device

BeatVault doesn't have a server, an account system, or a sign-in. Your music library, playlists, listening stats, theme choices, and profile picture are all stored locally on your phone (Android's app storage) and are never uploaded anywhere.

File access

BeatVault asks for storage/file permissions so it can find, play, and edit tags (title, artist, key, etc.) on the audio files you point it at via "Add folder" / "Add song(s)". It only reads and writes files inside folders you've explicitly registered - it doesn't scan your whole device or send file contents off the phone.

The diagnostic log

BeatVault keeps a rolling, on-device-only log of player events (play/pause/seek, that kind of thing) for up to 24 hours, used to help debug problems. It never leaves your phone on its own. The only way it goes anywhere is if you choose Support > Report a Bug, which opens your own email app with the log attached as a file - you see and control that email before it sends, and it goes to the address below.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

BeatVault doesn't include any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK. Nothing about how you use the app is collected or sent anywhere - there's simply no code path that does that.

Trial timer

BeatVault includes a 14-day free trial. Its start date is stored in a small file on your device, kept separate from your song library so it survives an uninstall/reinstall. When online, the app also makes one request to a public time service (worldtimeapi.org) to check the real current time - this only guards against resetting the trial by changing your device's clock. No personal data is sent to or received from that request.

Online song details and covers

Only when you use Identify (ID) or Search Online in the song editor, BeatVault sends the search words you see (artist, title, album, year) to MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org) and downloads cover images from the Cover Art Archive (coverartarchive.org, hosted by archive.org). Some mobile networks can't reach archive.org, so if a cover fails to load the app fetches the same image through the wsrv.nl image service instead. Like any website, these services see your IP address, and wsrv.nl keeps its request logs for 7 days. No account, song file, or other personal data is sent.

Nothing is sold or shared

Since BeatVault doesn't collect data in the first place, there's nothing to sell or share with third parties.
